## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the standard process for opening individual and corporate deposit accounts across all branches.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all branch staff involved in account opening, including tellers, account officers, branch managers, and compliance liaisons.

## 3. Required Documents

### 3.1 Individual Account

| Document | Required | Accepted Alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| Government-issued photo ID (valid/unexpired) | Yes | Passport, Driver's License, National ID, Postal ID, PRC ID |
| Proof of address (within 3 months) | Yes | Utility bill, bank statement, barangay certificate |
| TIN or tax exemption certificate | Yes | BIR Form 1901/1902 |
| Completed Account Opening Form (AOF-01) | Yes | Digital AOF via tablet |
| Specimen signature card | Yes | Digital signature capture |
| Initial deposit | Yes | Per product minimum (see Section 5) |

### 3.2 Corporate / Business Account

| Document | Required |
|---|---|
| SEC/DTI/CDA Certificate of Registration | Yes |
| Articles of Incorporation / Partnership | Yes |
| Board Resolution or Secretary's Certificate authorizing signatories | Yes |
| Government-issued ID of authorized signatories | Yes |
| Business permit | Yes |
| TIN | Yes |
| Proof of business address | Yes |
| Completed Corporate AOF (AOF-02) | Yes |
| Beneficial ownership declaration (BOD-01) | Yes |

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Walk-in Account Opening

1. Greet the customer and determine account type needed.
2. Provide the appropriate Account Opening Form (AOF-01 or AOF-02).
3. Collect and verify all required documents per Section 3.
4. Verify government ID using the bank's ID verification system.
5. Capture specimen signature (digital or card).
6. Encode customer information in the core banking system.
7. Process initial deposit.
8. Issue account confirmation and welcome kit.
9. Provide disclosures: fee schedule, dormancy rules, data privacy notice.
10. File documents per records retention policy (POL-2024-010).

### 4.2 Digital Account Opening

1. Customer completes digital application via mobile app or website.
2. System performs automated ID verification and liveness check.
3. Customer uploads supporting documents.
4. Digital onboarding team reviews application within 1 business day.
5. If approved, account is activated and customer is notified.
6. If additional documents are needed, customer is notified via app/email.

## 5. Minimum Initial Deposit

| Product | Currency | Minimum Deposit |
|---|---|---:|
| Regular Savings | PHP | 2,000 |
| Regular Savings | USD | 200 |
| Premium Savings | PHP | 50,000 |
| Checking Account | PHP | 10,000 |
| Business Checking | PHP | 25,000 |
| Time Deposit | PHP | 50,000 |

## 6. Dormancy Rules

- Accounts with no customer-initiated transaction for 2 years are classified as dormant.
- Dormant accounts are subject to a monthly dormancy fee of PHP 30.
- Accounts with zero balance after dormancy fees are closed per BSP regulations.

## 7. Exceptions

- Minors (below 18) may open accounts with a parent/guardian as joint holder.
- Non-resident accounts require additional documentation per BSP Circular 1014.
- Trust accounts follow a separate SOP (POL-2024-015).
