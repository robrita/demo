## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the standard for receiving, documenting, investigating, and resolving customer complaints across all channels.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all branch staff, contact center agents, relationship managers, digital banking support teams, and back-office operations.

## 3. Complaint Classification

| Category | Code | SLA (Business Days) |
|---|---|---:|
| Service delay or error | CMP-SVC | 5 |
| Fee or charge dispute | CMP-FEE | 5 |
| Unauthorized transaction | CMP-UTX | 10 |
| Product / rate dispute | CMP-PRD | 7 |
| Staff conduct | CMP-STF | 10 |
| System / digital channel issue | CMP-SYS | 3 |
| Privacy or data concern | CMP-PRI | 5 |
| Regulatory or legal | CMP-REG | 10 |

## 4. Complaint Handling Procedure

### 4.1 Receipt and Acknowledgment

1. Log the complaint in the Complaint Management System (CMS) within 1 hour of receipt.
2. Issue a complaint reference number to the customer.
3. Acknowledge receipt to the customer within 1 business day via the customer's preferred channel.

### 4.2 Investigation

1. Assign complaint to the responsible department based on category.
2. Investigating officer reviews transaction records, system logs, and customer communications.
3. If the complaint involves a third party (merchant, partner bank), coordinate with the external relations team.
4. Document findings in the CMS.

### 4.3 Resolution

1. Determine resolution: refund, fee reversal, process correction, apology, or denial with explanation.
2. Obtain required approval per the Complaint Resolution Authority Matrix (Section 5).
3. Communicate resolution to the customer in writing.
4. If the customer is unsatisfied, inform them of the internal appeal process and external recourse (BSP Financial Consumer Protection Department).

### 4.4 Closure

1. Update CMS with final resolution, customer acceptance, and closure date.
2. Complaints unresolved after SLA trigger automatic escalation to the Division Head.

## 5. Complaint Resolution Authority Matrix

| Resolution Type | Maximum Value | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Fee reversal | PHP 5,000 | Branch Manager |
| Fee reversal | PHP 50,000 | Area Manager |
| Goodwill credit | PHP 10,000 | Division Head |
| Goodwill credit | > PHP 10,000 | SVP or above |
| Process correction | N/A | Department Head |
| Apology letter | N/A | Branch Manager or above |

## 6. Escalation Triggers

The following situations require immediate escalation to the Compliance team or senior management:

- Customer threatens legal action.
- Complaint involves potential regulatory breach.
- Complaint involves data privacy or unauthorized disclosure.
- Customer identifies as media, government official, or public figure.
- Complaint involves a vulnerable customer (elderly, disabled, low financial literacy).
- Complaint involves potential fraud or unauthorized access.

## 7. Reporting

- Monthly complaint report is submitted to the Customer Experience Committee.
- Root cause analysis is required for recurring complaints (3+ complaints on the same issue in 30 days).
- BSP reportable complaints are forwarded to the Compliance Division per BSP Circular 857.
