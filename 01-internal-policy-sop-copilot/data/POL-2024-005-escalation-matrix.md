## 1. Purpose

This document defines the escalation paths for operational, compliance, legal, fraud, and customer-impact scenarios.

## 2. Escalation Categories

### 2.1 Operational Escalation

| Trigger | Escalate To | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| Branch system outage > 1 hour | IT Operations + Area Manager | Immediately |
| Failed batch processing | IT Operations + Back-Office Head | Within 30 minutes |
| Cash shortage / excess > PHP 50,000 | Branch Manager + Cash Management | Immediately |
| ATM / CDM malfunction | IT Helpdesk + Channel Management | Within 1 hour |

### 2.2 Customer Complaint Escalation

| Trigger | Escalate To | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| Complaint SLA breach | Division Head | Automatic (system) |
| Customer threatens legal action | Legal Department + Compliance | Within 1 hour |
| Media-related complaint | Corporate Communications + Legal | Immediately |
| Vulnerable customer complaint | Customer Experience Head | Within 2 hours |

### 2.3 Compliance / Regulatory Escalation

| Trigger | Escalate To | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| Suspected money laundering | AMLC Reporting Officer | Within 1 hour |
| Unusual transaction per BSP threshold | Compliance Division | Same day |
| Customer reports unauthorized transaction | Fraud Operations + Compliance | Immediately |
| Data privacy breach suspected | Data Protection Office | Within 1 hour |
| Regulatory examination finding | Chief Compliance Officer | Same day |

### 2.4 Fraud Escalation

| Trigger | Escalate To | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed fraudulent transaction | Fraud Operations | Immediately |
| Suspected internal fraud | Internal Audit + HR | Within 1 hour |
| Phishing / social engineering attempt | Information Security + Fraud Ops | Immediately |
| Card skimming report | Fraud Operations + Channel Management | Immediately |

### 2.5 Legal Escalation

| Trigger | Escalate To | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| Court order or subpoena received | Legal Department | Within 1 hour |
| Customer files formal legal action | Legal + Compliance | Within 2 hours |
| Regulatory penalty or sanction notice | Legal + CCO + CEO | Immediately |

## 3. Escalation Procedure

1. Staff identifies the escalation trigger per the tables above.
2. Staff notifies the immediate supervisor verbally and logs the escalation in the Incident Management System (IMS).
3. The designated escalation contact acknowledges receipt within the specified timeframe.
4. The escalation owner provides status updates every 4 hours (or as agreed) until resolution.
5. Final resolution is documented in the IMS and root cause analysis is completed within 5 business days.

## 4. After-Hours Escalation

- Critical escalations (fraud, system outage, regulatory) use the 24/7 Operations Hotline: ext. 8888.
- The on-duty Operations Manager coordinates with the relevant department head.
